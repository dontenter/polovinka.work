# Brazil district comparison profiles

Snapshot checked 2026-10-24, tailored to homes and apartments with exactly 3 bedrooms. Data: `lib/brazil-rental-evidence.json`. Sources: linked Viva Real neighborhood/type listing pages, supplemented by cited Chaves na Mão, OLX and Imovelweb listings where the visible sample was thin. Retain item-level price, floor area, street, bedrooms, and additional source URL.

Monthly asking rents, not signed contract prices. Up to five first visible eligible listings per source page, not random or exhaustive. Reject explicit seasonal/daily/sale offers, grouped advertisements, wrong neighborhoods, other room counts and missing addresses; deduplicate same street+floor area conservatively. Neighbor recommendations appear within portal lists and must be excluded. Monthly advertisements can still be seasonal; annual availability, term and furnishing remain unconfirmed and are disclosed. Never substitute overall neighborhood/type averages for a three-bedroom comparison. Values exclude separately charged condominium, IPTU and utilities. Means are arithmetic, rounded to BRL 100; ranges retain observed prices. n=1 is identified as one offer, not a district average. Sample composition and source inventory may change; snapshot is manually maintained.

Lifestyle criteria are provisional editorial interpretations of physical geography, source neighborhood descriptions and named facilities, not empirical indices or resident survey results. Scale 1–5, higher means more convenient; for traffic, higher means fewer expected delays, not measured travel speed. Do not produce an overall score. Safety remains null: the reviewed SSP/SC statewide bulletin and capital-level ranking do not establish a comparable set of district crime rates. Missing safety data is not evidence of safety or danger.

Additional sources: ACATE institutional center addresses (https://www.acate.com.br/institucional/), municipal Parque de Coqueiros facilities (https://ponteviva.pmf.sc.gov.br/esporte-e-lazer.html), municipal Jardim Botânico project (https://redeplanejamento.pmf.sc.gov.br/pt-BR/projetos/jardim-botanico). Official safety bulletin: https://ssp.sc.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/03-marco-2026.pdf. These sources do not assign the editorial numerical scores.

Nearest school = minimum Haversine distance from each existing district reference pin to the four existing approximate international/bilingual school points. This is not distance from a selected home, district average, road length, travel time, or proof of enrollment eligibility. Directions link uses the known school street address for route checking.
