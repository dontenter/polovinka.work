# Brazil map activities

Initial shortlist expanded into an incomplete venue directory. Original 12-venue shortlist, checked 2026-10-24: four kids activities, three strength gyms, two padel clubs and three court-tennis venues. Canonical records and primary source links: `lib/brazil-activities.ts`. This is not an exhaustive directory or confirmation of availability in 2027. No pricing copied from promotions; the BRL/USD provider remains available for existing prices.

## Location provenance

Smart Fit coordinates come from each unit’s published geo.position/map URL; Sotália and LIC (Movi’s host) from their official businessLocationCoordinates. Pé de Ballet and Just Play use their published map embeds (approximate viewport coordinates). Tennis Health publishes its map marker coordinates. JUSC uses the OSM/Nominatim named facility result. OnTennis and Jurerê Padel use OSM street reference points, explicitly described as such. CPAteliê uses the OSM street reference within Cachoeira do Bom Jesus, not the unrelated same-road result in Canasvieiras. Logosófico is an approximate school location (public school-directory coordinates); its program and address are sourced from the school’s own page. All navigation links search by the published venue name and address, not just the approximate pin.

Jurerê Padel’s official page describes two courts and lessons; street number is corroborated by the Super Padel and Padelmesh directories. Children’s programs are leads for inquiry, with no promise of a suitable age group, available places or acceptance of non-enrolled schoolchildren.

## Expanded coverage, 2026-10-24

Imported every open Florianópolis branch in Pratique’s public directory array (15; `em_breve=0`), all three Florianópolis branches in Engenharia do Corpo’s unit catalog, and the additional Smart Fit Agronômica, Floripa Shopping and Estreito branches. These coordinates are published by the operators. Added Stronger Fit in Canasvieiras (official Linktree; street address corroborated by ILoveFloripa), and Access in Lagoa (third-party mirror; explicitly marked for confirmation). Their coordinates locate OSM streets, not doors.

Added H2 on the mainland from its official Linktree map. Lemon in Campeche and ELASE in Pantanal explicitly describe padel on their own current sites; Lemon also offers tennis, ELASE tennis and a member-only gym. They are single records with multiple categories, never duplicate facility pins. ELASE location comes from official JSON-LD. Lemon and Clube 93 locations are approximate published map embeds. Clube 93’s club/address are confirmed, but padel is only described in third-party listings and older event coverage; its card must explicitly require confirmation.

Not added as active clubs: Cachoeira Padel (closure conflict), historical Clube do Rei and P2 entries (old seven-digit phone numbers), IN Padel (unverified directory lead), Floripa Padel in neighboring São José (outside this municipality), or the seasonal airport court (coverage only through March 2026). Do not claim the directory is exhaustive. Scope controls distinguish island and mainland; category numbers are mapped records within the chosen scope, not a city census.
